At least since 2005, the Swiss Mathematical Olympiad is also welcoming participants from Liechtenstein.
Officially, they participate as *guests*, with independent qualification criteria that are less codified.

The Swiss Mathematical Olympiad is also charged with representing Liechtenstein at the International Mathematical Olympiad.
Note that in some years, the selection of the team was done independently of the Swiss Mathematical Olympiad, so the data on this website regarding Liechtenstein is not very complete.

For the purpose of this archive, the following conventions are followed:

- Guest participants are fully included in the ranking of any exam they participate in
- Guest participants are not awarded medals
- Qualification of guest participants to subsequent rounds is dealt with on a case-by-case basis
