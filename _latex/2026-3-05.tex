We call a strictly increasing sequence $a_1,a_2,\dots, a_{2026}$ of positive integers
\emph{indivisible} if for all $2\leq k\leq 2026$,
the sum of any $k$ consecutive terms is \textbf{not} divisible by $k$.
    
Prove that an indivisible sequence exists and
determine the smallest possible value of the last term in an indivisible sequence.
