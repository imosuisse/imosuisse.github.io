Determine all functions \(f\colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Z}\) with the following property: for every positive integer \(n\) and every positive divisor \(a\) of \(n\) there exists a positive divisor \(b\) of \(n\) such that \[n \mid a + f(b).\]
