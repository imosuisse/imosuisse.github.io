Lord Shen and Po are playing a game on a regular \(2026\)-gon. Lord Shen writes a positive real number in red at each vertex, such that the product of the \(2026\) red numbers (which are not necessarily distinct) is \(1\).

Then, Po draws non-intersecting line segments between the vertices of the \(2026\)-gon, in a way that each created region is a triangle. Inside each of these triangles, he writes out the product of the three red numbers written on its vertices in green.

Determine the largest real number \(c\) such that, no matter the numbers chosen by Lord Shen, Po can make sure that the sum of the green numbers is at least \(c\).
