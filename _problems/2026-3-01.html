Let \(n\) be a positive integer. Archi and Baldibus are constructing a number on a blackboard, one digit at a time. To start, Archi writes down one of the nine digits from \(1\) to \(9\). Then, starting with Baldibus, the two players alternate the following:

  • Baldibus appends one of the ten digits from \(0\) to \(9\) to the end of the current number.

  • Archi prepends one of the nine digits from \(1\) to \(9\) to the front of the current number.

Archi wins if, immediately after Archi writes a digit, the number on the board is divisible by \(n\). Determine the values of \(n\) for which Archi can guarantee a win, no matter what Baldibus does.
