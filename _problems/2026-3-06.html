Let \(\Gamma_1\) and \(\Gamma_2\) be two circles tangent at \(T\), such that \(\Gamma_2\) is inside of \(\Gamma_1\). Let \(PQ\) be a chord of \(\Gamma_1\), tangent to \(\Gamma_2\) at \(C\). Point \(A\neq T\) lies on \(\Gamma_1\) and \(AT\) intersects \(\Gamma_2\) at \(B \neq T\). Let line \(BC\) intersect \(AP\) and \(AQ\) at \(X\) and \(Y\), respectively. Prove that \(TB\) bisects the angle \(\angle XTY\).
