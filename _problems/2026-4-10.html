Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle. Let \(I_B\) and \(I_C\) be the excenters of triangle \(ABC\) opposite \(B\) and \(C\), respectively. Let \(E\) be a point on line \(BA\) such that \(\angle EI_BB=90^\circ\). Let \(F\) be a point on line \(CA\) such that \(\angle CI_C F=90^\circ\). Let the circle with center \(I_B\) and radius \(I_BE\) intersect the segment \(I_BB\) at \(X\). Similarly, let the circle with center \(I_C\) and radius \(I_CF\) intersect the segment \(I_CC\) at \(Y\).

Prove that \(BY\) is perpendicular to \(CX\).

Remark: The excenter of a triangle \(ABC\) opposite vertex \(B\) is the center of the circle that is tangent to line segment \(AC\), to ray \(BA\) beyond \(A\), and to ray \(BC\) beyond \(C\). The excenter opposite \(C\) is similarly defined.
