Heidi is placing Ricola on an empty \(2026 \times 2026\) grid. At each step, Heidi chooses a row or column in which the total number of Ricola is a multiple of \(3\), chooses an empty cell in this row or column, and places a single Ricola in it. If there is no such row or column, Heidi stops.

Determine the maximum number of Ricola that Heidi can place.
