Let \(n,k \geq 2\) and \(a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_k\) be positive integers satisfying \(n < k < 2n\) and \[2^n - 1\mid 2^{a_1} + 2^{a_2} + \cdots + 2^{a_k}.\] Show that at least three of \(a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_k\) have the same remainder when divided by \(n\).
