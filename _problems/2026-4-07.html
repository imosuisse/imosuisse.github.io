Let \(n\) be a positive integer. Determine all sequences of integers \(1 \leq a_1 \leq \dots \leq a_n\) such that

  • each \(a_i\) is a power of two, and

  • \(\left( 2^{a_1} + 1\right)(2^{a_2}+1) \cdots \left( 2^{a_n} + 1\right) = 2^{1 + a_1 + \dots + a_n} - 1.\)
