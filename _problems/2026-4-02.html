Let \(A,B,X,C\) be points on a circle \(\omega\) in this order, such that \(AX > AB, AC\). Let \(P, Q \neq X\) be points on lines \(XB\) and \(XC\) respectively, such that \(AP = AX = AQ\). Let \(M\) and \(N\) denote the midpoints of \(BC\) and \(PQ\) respectively. If \(S \neq A\) is the second intersection of \(NA\) with \(\omega\), prove that \(MN = MS\).
