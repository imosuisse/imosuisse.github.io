Let \(\Omega\) be a circle with diameter \([AB]\) and \(C\) a point on the segment \([AB]\). Let \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_2\) be two lines, such that the projections of \(C\) onto them both lie on \(\Omega\). Let \(W\) and \(X\) be intersections of the tangent to \(\Omega\) at \(A\) with \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_2\) respectively. Let \(Y\) and \(Z\) be intersections of the tangent to \(\Omega\) at \(B\) with \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_2\) respectively. Prove that \(WZ\), \(XY\) and \(AB\) concur at a point.
